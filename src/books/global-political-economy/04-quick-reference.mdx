## Session 1: stakeholders and trust

| Concept | Remember |
|---|---|
| Trust | A stakeholder's willingness to be **vulnerable** to a business (Pirson & Malhotra, 2011) |
| Three core dimensions | **Ability** (can deliver) · **Benevolence** (genuinely cares) · **Integrity** (fair and honest) |
| Three signals | Voluntary action · Transparency · Responsiveness |
| Voluntary action signals | **Benevolence** |
| Transparency signals | **Integrity** |
| Responsiveness signals | **Ability + Benevolence** |
| P1 | Action toward **one** stakeholder sends trust cues to **all** |
| P5 | **Proximity** amplifies the effect |
| P6 (key) | **Prioritising** stakeholders **lowers** trust |
| Freeman (1984) | A fix forced by a court or government = **managerial failure** |

## Session 2: investment and PEST

| Concept | Remember |
|---|---|
| Four FDI motives | Resources · Markets · Efficiency · Strategic assets |
| Six country risks | Political · Economic · Transfer · Exchange rate · Location/Neighbourhood · Sovereign |
| Horizontal vs vertical FDI | Horizontal copies the chain (local risk); vertical splits it (global risk) |
| PEST golden rule | Direct bearing on the industry **and** likely to change |
| PESTEL / PESTLE | + Environmental + Legal |
| STEEPLE | + Ethics (CSR lens) |
| STEEPLED | + Demographics (most comprehensive) |
| STEER | + Regulatory |
| PEST in SWOT | Gives **O and T** only |

## Session 3: geopolitics

| Concept | Remember |
|---|---|
| Three events | New nationalism and trade wars · Covid-19 · Regional conflicts |
| India–China trade deficit | **USD 112.60 billion**, 2025-26 |
| GSP withdrawn for India | **2019** |
| WTO problem | US blocking Appellate Body appointments |
| JIT → JIC | Lean, no buffer → safety stock |
| Komatsu · Apple · ABB · Murata | Reshoring · Just-in-Case · Supplier digitisation · Near-shoring to Thailand |
| AUKUS | Australia · United Kingdom · United States |
| McKinsey Trade & Economics | Trade agreements · Import/export controls · Domestic policies · Tariffs · Industrial policy |
| McKinsey Security | FDI restrictions · Sanctions · Alliances · Conflict · Tech/IP/cyber controls |
| Geopolitics | How geography affects politics and relations between states (Caldara & Iacoviello, 2022) |
| LIO | Liberal International Order: built by the US and allies after WWII on free trade and multilateral institutions |
| Three phases | Emerging (pre-2008) → Approval (2008–2019) → Expansion (2019 on) |
| Six levels | Individual → Firm → Sub-national → National → Supra-national → Global |
| Four views | Inside-In → Inside-Out → Outside-In → **Outside-Out** (goal) |
| Bouncing with | **Pre-emptive**: the big setback never lands |
| Fat tails | Not rare, just under-weighted (Russia's invasion) |
| Turkey analogy | 1,000 safe days, then Thanksgiving |
| Six resilience dimensions | Reputational · Organisational · Financial · Technological · Operational · Business model |
